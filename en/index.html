03 / OUR APPROACH

Trust needs
evidence.

Serious research starts with good questions. Trustworthy technology requires answers that can be checked.

A+
  1. 01

    Evidence before conclusions.

    A signal calls for investigation. A vulnerability requires validation of context, reachability and impact. Our public tools make that distinction explicit.

  2. 02

    Authorization sets the scope.

    Security research belongs on systems you own or have permission to test. Clear boundaries are part of the technical work.

  3. 03

    Make limitations visible.

    Releases, pre-releases and research have different levels of maturity. Code and documentation help you evaluate what each tool delivers.

  4. 04

    AI with responsibility.

    Our focus is to support human analysis, with critical evaluation of results and respect for confidentiality.
